Questions parents & students ask

Before you take the test

Is this an entrance exam?

No. It is a career-fit assessment designed to help you understand whether Merchant Navy life and its major career tracks fit you.

Does the assessment guarantee a Merchant Navy job?

No. It does not guarantee admission, employment or a Certificate of Competency. It is designed to support a better-informed career decision.

What if I'm confused between Deck, Engine and ETO?

That is one of the central questions the assessment addresses. Your report compares your fit across the three tracks and provides direction for what to explore next.

What if I haven't chosen my course yet?

That's a good time to take it. The report is designed to help you think about the career track before you commit to a particular training route.

How long does it take?

Approximately 30–40 minutes. Set aside uninterrupted time so your responses reflect what you would genuinely do rather than what sounds ideal.
