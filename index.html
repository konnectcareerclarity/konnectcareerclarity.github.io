Questions

Before you start

Which grade should I choose?

Choose your child's current grade. The assessment is slightly shorter for Grades 8 and 9, and the guidance in the report is matched to the decisions coming next.

How long does the assessment take?

About 40 to 50 minutes for Grades 8 and 9, and 50 to 60 for Grades 10 to 12. Answers are saved on the same device, so the student can pause and return with the same link.

Who should take it, the student or the parent?

The student, alone and in a quiet place. Parents join later, when the family reads the report together and in the session.

When do we get the report?

Ritu reviews every report before it is sent, so it reaches you by email after you finish the assessment. Then you can book your included 30-minute session.

What is in the 30-minute session?

A one-to-one conversation with Ritu about the report: stream or course choices, colleges, entrance exams and your next steps. Parents are welcome to join.

Is this an IQ test or a pass/fail exam?

No. It looks at interests, strengths, learning style and values. It does not label a child, and it does not decide a career. It gives the family a clear starting point.
