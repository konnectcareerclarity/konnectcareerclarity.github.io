Questions

Before you start

Is it too late in Grade 12?

No. If applications and entrance exams are still ahead, the report helps you shortlist courses and plan what to do first.

Does the report cover studying abroad?

The report lists well-known options in India. Because rules, entrance tests and costs differ by country and change every year, studying abroad is covered in a separate 30-minute counselling session with Ritu, free of cost. Message us on WhatsApp to book it.

How long does the assessment take?

About 50 to 60 minutes. Answers are saved on the same device, so your child can pause and return with the same link.

Should my child take it alone?

Yes, alone and in a quiet place, so the answers are honest. Parents join later, when the family reads the report together and in the 30-minute session.

Is this an IQ test or a marks-based exam?

No. It looks at interests, strengths, learning style and values. It does not label a child and does not decide a career.

How do we get the report?

After your child finishes, Ritu reviews the report and it reaches you by email as a PDF. You can then book your included 30-minute session.

Which grade do we choose when we start?

On the first screen of the test, choose Grade 12. The same ₹2,999 applies to every grade.
