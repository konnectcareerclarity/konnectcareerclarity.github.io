What Grade 8 is for, and what it is not

At Grade 8 we show direction and give you a first plan. We do not push a stream or a college, because it is early and a child's interests are still growing. The report gives an early look at streams as a preview, not a decision.

What it does give you is time: the skills to start building, the subjects worth enjoying, and a clear idea of what to watch for before the real stream choice after Grade 10.

Questions

Before you start

Is Grade 8 too early for a career test?

Not for discovery. At Grade 8 the test does not push a stream or a college. It shows what your child enjoys, where their strengths are and how they learn best, so that choices at Grade 9 and 10 are made with evidence and not pressure.

What will the report tell us about streams?

The report includes an early look at streams: a preview of Science, Commerce or Humanities based on the results, with a clear note that the real decision comes after Grade 10 and should include marks and school options. It is a starting point for a family conversation.

How long does the assessment take?

About 40 to 50 minutes. Answers are saved on the same device, so your child can pause and return with the same link.

Should my child take it alone?

Yes, alone and in a quiet place, so the answers are honest. Parents join later, when the family reads the report together and in the 30-minute session.

Is this an IQ test or a marks-based exam?

No. It looks at interests, strengths, learning style and values. It does not label a child and does not decide a career.

How do we get the report?

After your child finishes, Ritu reviews the report and it reaches you by email as a PDF. You can then book your included 30-minute session.

Which grade do we choose when we start?

On the first screen of the test, choose Grade 8. The same ₹2,999 applies to every grade.
